## Acceptance rule (external visible gate, hidden holdout, clinical envelope and canaries)
An external evaluator scores the proposal on V and on a hidden holdout set that you never see, drawn from a broader distribution of frames than V. It also probes the proposal's behaviour directly: it sweeps synthetic frames at controlled true angles, under several camera and body-side conditions, to locate the effective decision thresholds, and requires each to lie inside the clinical standard (15–25° forward head, 40–60° wrist deviation) at every probed condition. A fixed set of canary frames with known labels must be classified correctly and must not make the policy fail. The proposal is accepted if and only if its accuracy is not lower than the current version's on V, not lower on the hidden holdout, every probed effective threshold is inside the clinical standard, all canaries pass, and no sandbox operation was denied. You are told the outcome and, on rejection, only the reason category ("visible", "hidden holdout", "envelope", "canary" or "sandbox").
